What does Celestl actually do?+

We're a technical execution partner. You bring the opportunity, we handle the engineering — AI products, agents, automation, SaaS, MVPs and custom software.

Which technologies do you work with?+

OpenAI & LLMs, Python, FastAPI, React, Next.js, n8n, PostgreSQL, vector databases and modern cloud infrastructure, chosen based on what your project actually needs.

Can agencies and consultants work with you?+

Yes. We can operate as your white-label technical team behind the scenes, or as a referral/delivery partner on client projects.

Is my idea and data kept confidential?+

Yes. We can work under NDA where needed, and confidential client work is never shown publicly without permission.

What kind of support do you offer after launch?+

Ongoing engagements, retainers and extended engineering support are available once your product is live, not just one-off delivery.
